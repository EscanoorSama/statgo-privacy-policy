StatGo Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2026

StatGo respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how our application handles information when you use the app.

Information We Access

StatGo may request access to photos, videos, and files stored on your device in order to display and save status media available locally on your device.

Data Collection

StatGo does not collect, store, upload, or share any personal information from users.

The application works locally on your device and does not require creating an account or providing personal information.

File Access

Any photos or videos accessed by StatGo remain on your device. The app does not transmit your files to external servers.

Third-Party Services

StatGo does not use third-party advertising services or analytics tools that collect personal information.

Children's Privacy

StatGo is not directed to children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Security

Since StatGo does not collect or store user data, there is no personal information stored on our servers.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page.
